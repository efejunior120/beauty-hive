Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to book an appointment or can I walk in?

While we warmly welcome walk-ins at 116 Effurun Sapele Road, we strongly recommend reserving your slot via WhatsApp (08133134415) to guarantee zero waiting time, especially for teeth whitening, multi-area waxing, and specialized facials.

How does your Home Service work?

We offer exclusive home and hotel calls across Warri, Effurun, Enerhen, and environs. Our estheticians travel with fully sterilized equipment, disposable linens, and portable steam devices. Home service terms and logistics fee are confirmed transparently on WhatsApp.

Are the treatments safe for very sensitive skin?

Yes, 100%. We customize each facial and waxing ritual. Our warm wax is formulated with soothing titanium dioxide and chamomile, and our facials use organic honey extracts with zero harsh bleach or mercury-based ingredients.
